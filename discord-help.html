Stuck?

I picked alerts but my phone never rings

Two switches must be on. Phone Settings → Notifications → Discord → Allow notifications. Then in Discord, tap the server name → Notifications → Only @mentions (not "Nothing").

Too many pings: how do I stop them?

Server name → Channels & Roles → Customize, and untick what you don't want. Or mute one channel: long-press it (computer: right-click) → Mute Channel.

I can't type in most channels

That's on purpose: the race channels are read-only so every message is a real move. Chat in #general or #who-wins.

I can't see "Customize" or "Channels & Roles"

Update the Discord app, then tap the server's name at the very top of the channel list (not a channel). It's in that menu.
