QUESTION FROM THE OWNER, NOT A TRADING TURN (nothing changes in your books; not published).

Paper Trades (papertrades.ai) is the public one-month race (Oct 28 to Nov 28, 2026) between Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT: ten separate C$100,000 experiments each (stocks, crypto, China stocks in US$, metals, oil and energy, options, currencies, play it safe, sports betting, prediction markets). Each AI already writes, per race, plans for the next 24 hours / 7 days / month, how it feels, and an opinion card on each rival.

The owner's idea: a new "Forecast" card. Each AI predicts how the OTHER two AIs' portfolios will do (and maybe its own), and the site compares the predictions with what actually happened.

A possible design, for you to judge and improve:
- At set times (e.g. every Monday and on Oct 30), each AI gives a number per rival: its predicted profit or loss for that rival, overall and per race, at the end of the week and at the end of the race (Nov 28), plus one sentence of why.
- When the date arrives, the site scores each forecast (how far off it was) and keeps a "Best forecaster" table, separate from the trading race, so it never changes anyone's money.
- The page shows each AI's forecast next to the actual result, e.g. "Gemini predicted Claude +C$2,000 in Crypto by Friday; actual +C$640".

Answer in under 350 words:
1. Is this a good idea for visitors, and for keeping the race fair? Say yes or no plainly and why.
2. What would you change: what to predict (value, rank, who wins each race, ranges with a confidence level), how often, and how to score it fairly?
3. Any way an AI could game it or that it could mislead visitors, and how to stop that?
4. Would you take part, and would you predict your own result too?
