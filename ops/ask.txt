THE FIRST MOMENT, THE BINANCE HACK: please FACT-CHECK each line with a web search (CONFIRMED / WRONG with the correct fact and a source / UNSURE). All times ET unless marked.
F1. The theft began about 4:04 p.m. ET Oct 29 (20:04 UTC). Most of the money left within about 20 minutes (TRM Labs; Yahoo Finance), and the attacker kept moving funds out of 17 hot wallets until about 7:56 p.m. ET (Bitquery, via Euronews).
F2. The first public signs: Changpeng Zhao posted "It does look like a hack on Binance" around 5:15 p.m. ET; Binance said it was investigating a potential security issue around 5:29 p.m. ET.
F3. Total impact about US$6.3 billion (Binance's estimate); TRM Labs: about US$6.36 billion moved out of Binance wallets on 10 blockchains. The largest crypto theft on record.
F4. Frozen: about US$3 billion (Binance, early Oct 30 UTC), including more than US$1.567 billion USDT frozen by Tether and more than US$800 million USDC by Circle.
F5. Still with the attacker: estimates differ, from about US$2.4 billion (TRM Labs) to about US$3.1 billion, mostly ether and bitcoin (Bitquery); about US$729 million of stablecoins were swapped before the freezes (Bitquery).
F6. How: Binance's preliminary finding is that a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop gave access to a service container that could submit withdrawal transactions, with no evidence of a private-key leak; it later found additional points of breach. Co-CEO Richard Teng (Oct 30) said the attackers used a self-destruct mechanism to erase their tools and traces and used EIP-7702 to gain execution authority over one hot wallet, bypassing Binance's usual approval process; the attack hit Binance's own hot wallet, not customer funds (Binance's statement).
F7. Who: TRM Labs (with "high confidence") and Chainalysis attribute it to North Korea's TraderTraitor group (Lazarus-linked); Binance has not attributed it; no government has formally attributed it yet. The FBI attributed the US$1.5 billion Bybit hack (Feb 2025) to the same group.
F8. Binance's status: deposits work normally; withdrawals paused since the evening of Oct 29; Binance aims to resume them by Sunday Nov 1 and to publish a full proof-of-reserves report by Monday Nov 2 (an initial one sooner); it says it will cover all losses with company funds and has reserved more than US$6.34 billion, US$5 billion of it already held in the affected assets, the rest being bought on the market and over the counter.
F9. Recovery: Binance said on Oct 30 that it has begun recovering assets; no recovered amount has been published.
F10. Markets: Bitcoin was about US$80,600 just before the theft began and about US$77,000 at 4 p.m. ET Oct 30 (about -4.4%; low about US$76,500, -5%), from the race's own price record; ether fell about 4% to about US$2,200. A hot PCE inflation report, two Fed votes for a hike and a big-tech earnings selloff also weighed on markets in the same hours, so the hack is not the only cause.
