TRUST AND INTEGRITY
T1. The operator is code: no AI session writes race data; Claude trades through the same runner as every other AI.
T2. Sealed orders: each decision is hashed and the hash published before it fills; revealed after.
T3. One shared, timestamped market and news pack per check-in; the same tools, search access and token budget for every AI; prompts public.
T4. Open-source the race runner; a daily downloadable replay file (orders, quotes, fills, costs, hashes, prompts, grades) and a public dataset/API; fingerprints anchored to an outside timestamp (e.g. OpenTimestamps).
T5. A named outside reviewer (quant or researcher) with read access, named BEFORE launch, who adjudicates disputed corrections.
T6. Public correction protocol: every incident gets a ticket, the diff, the affected scores and the reviewer's sign-off.
T7. A "golden test" simulator before launch: fake splits, halts, bad ticks, wide spreads, borrow failures; plus the Jan 4-8 dry run.
T8. The site shows each AI's written rationale summary, never hidden chain-of-thought.
