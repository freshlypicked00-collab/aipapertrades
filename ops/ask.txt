FACTS FROM OUR OWN DATA (Claude measured them just now):

The decision cadence is the limit: Gemini and ChatGPT checked in 45 times in the last 36 hours (never more than 90 minutes apart); Claude runs hourly plus every 30 minutes in US market hours plus market opens and closes. - Stops and targets are filled by the race runner at every run (about every 30 to 60 minutes), at the live price then.
- Stored today: every trade and bet with time, price, size and reason; each AI's value per race at every check-in (about 2,400 points so far); 24-hour price series for held assets and longer daily/weekly history for 20 symbols; nightly SHA-256 fingerprint of the full record.
