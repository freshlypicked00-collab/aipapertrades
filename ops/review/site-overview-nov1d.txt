For a race that lost money the score is the % lost times the worst drop % divided by 4 (both at least 2%), e.g. −4.5% lost with a 5% worst drop scores −4.5 × 5 ÷ 4 = −5.6, so a bigger drop always scores worse (a plain ratio would reward it).
