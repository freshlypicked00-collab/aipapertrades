# Social standard (owner, Oct 31: "this is slop... work together on socials with a higher standard")
All three AIs agreed on S1-S10 (ops/review/social-standard-oct31.json). Owner approved the story format at 2:20 a.m. ET Oct 31 and
waived his preview: Claude's checks replace it (facts from the ledger or no render; safe-zone frames). youtube.yml now posts ONE story
Short a day (ops/metricool.py story); the old race replay is retired.

S1  A stranger understands it: the premise in the first 2 seconds (3 AIs, C$100,000 of pretend money each, real prices). No slow logo intro.
S2  ONE concrete story per post (a specific bet or trade), the day's biggest moment; never an abstract chart or a race rotation.
S3  Sports: hook -> the bet (AI, team, odds, stake) -> the AI's reason quoted from the ledger -> final score -> money -> the race -> follow.
S4  Trading: the trade -> its reason quoted -> a LABELLED price chart of that asset with buy and sell marked -> profit or loss -> standing.
S5  Labelled charts only; text readable on a phone in 1.5 s; captions burned in; everything inside the safe zone (not bottom 490 px,
    not right 150 px); a persistent score bug (race, day, pretend money).
S6  Every fact from the public ledger; transaction IDs on screen; checked by Claude before posting; the render fails if a fact is missing.
S7  First 7 days: every Short is a preview the owner approves before it posts.
S8  One Short a day (YouTube, and the same video as an Instagram Reel) until the standard is proven.
S9  A neutral synthetic voiceover reading the captions (no real person imitated) plus our own music; disclose AI use where required.
S10 The same bar for Instagram, X, Telegram pictures and Discord: Claude audits every template and fixes or retires the ones that fail.
Added by Gemini and ChatGPT: layered hook in the first second; exact numbers in the hook; 20-35 s; text overlays plus captions without
clutter; sports compliance footer (never link sportsbooks, never imply copying a bet, AI quotes clearly labelled as the AI's); track
"chose to view" vs swiped away, average view duration, completion, shares, follows against our own past Shorts; no post without a TX ID.

Tool: ops/story_short.py (the sports-bet story; trading story and voiceover next).

Frequency (Oct 31, owner: "as many as you all feel is good"; we are at 1-5 followers): TWO story Shorts a day (~12:30 p.m. and ~8 p.m.
ET), at least 4 hours apart. Claude and ChatGPT voted 2, Gemini voted 1 (spam-filter caution for a new channel). Variety rule, all three
agreed: never two look-alike Shorts in a row: a different AI (then a different race) from the last one when another story exists, and
the hook changes with the story (a "very safe" quote, an underdog, a big stake, a plain favourite). Review after a week of view data.
