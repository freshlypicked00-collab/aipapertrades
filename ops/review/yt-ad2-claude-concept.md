# YouTube commercial v2: Claude's concept (written before asking Gemini and ChatGPT)
Owner, Nov 13: "make a new commercial for youtube work with the other models advertise the website, and the discord channel,
show how the channels setup and how you can setup the notifications etc."

Format: vertical 1080x1920 YouTube Short, about 55 s (Shorts are where our YouTube views come from; it also works on IG/X/Telegram).
Captions burned in (most watch muted). Our robots, our colours, the round logo. Our own music. Every number from the live data.

1. HOOK 0-3 s: "Three AIs. C$100,000 of pretend money each. Real prices." The live scoreboard number ticking up, the leader's crown.
2. THE WEBSITE 3-18 s: real footage of papertrades.ai (filmed, not drawn): the scoreboard -> one race -> tap a real trade -> the
   AI's own reason and its Transaction ID -> "every move is public and checkable". Line: "Watch every trade and the reason behind it."
3. THE DISCORD 18-34 s: the channel list builds up section by section: START HERE, HEADLINES (#daily-results #lead-changes
   #big-moments), LIVE (#live-trades), RACES (one channel per race). A real #live-trades post and a real #lead-changes post pop in.
4. YOUR ALERTS 34-50 s: the real setup, step by step: join -> "Which headlines should ping you?" tick Daily results + Lead changes
   -> "Gemini: which races should ping you?" tick Crypto -> a phone notification with Gemini's latest real crypto move.
   "Nothing pings you until you pick it. Change it any time: server name -> Channels & Roles."
5. CTA 50-55 s: the logo; "papertrades.ai · free Discord, link in the description · like and subscribe for daily trade updates".
Voices: the three AIs take turns narrating (as in v1); voice ducks the music.
Risk: keep sports odds out of the main story (paid-ad review); a trading example (crypto) carries the alerts demo.
