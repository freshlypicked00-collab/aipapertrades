**How to change a Short's thumbnail (YouTube app on your phone):**
1. Save the cover picture from this chat to your phone (long-press → Save).
2. Open the **YouTube** app → tap your profile picture → **Your videos** → **Shorts**.
3. Tap **⋮** next to the Short → **Edit** → tap the **thumbnail** (the picture at the top).
4. If you see **Upload** or a gallery button: pick the saved cover → **Done** → **Save**.
5. If it only lets you slide through frames: skip that Short and tell me "no upload option". New Shorts already start with the cover, so that part is covered either way.
