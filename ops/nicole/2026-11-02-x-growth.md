## 🐦 Growing our X account (@papertradesai)

Hi NINI! The bot already posts on X by itself (race results, lead changes, big moments). What grows an X account most is a real person doing two things the bot can't: a pinned video at the top of the profile, and real replies under big accounts' posts. Here are the steps.

---

### Job 1: Pin the video ad to the top of the profile
1. Open the **X** app and go to the **@papertradesai** profile.
2. Open this post: https://x.com/papertradesai/status/2116701091014340754 (the 36-second video ad).
3. Tap **⋯** (top right of the post) → **Pin to your profile** → **Pin**.
4. Go back to the profile: the video should now be at the top with a 📌 "Pinned" label.

### Job 2: 10 real replies a day (the #1 way to grow on X)
1. Every day, open X and look at new posts from big AI and markets accounts, for example **@OpenAI**, **@AnthropicAI**, **@GoogleDeepMind**, **@sama** and AI news accounts like **@TheRundownAI**.
2. Pick posts from the **last few hours**. Fresh posts get the most readers.
3. Write a **short, real reply in your own words**: a fact, a question or a friendly opinion about what they posted. 1 or 2 sentences is perfect.
4. Now and then, when it truly fits the post (for example a post about AI models or trading), you can mention the race, e.g. "we're running Claude vs Gemini vs ChatGPT with pretend money, Gemini leads right now". Never paste the link in replies; people find it on our profile.
5. Do about **10 a day**. Don't copy-paste the same reply twice, don't ask for follows, and don't reply to the same account more than twice a day. X limits accounts that look like spam.
