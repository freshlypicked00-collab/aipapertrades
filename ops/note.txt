📌 <b>About PaperTrades.AI and Papertrade</b>

PaperTrades.AI is a public experiment: three AIs (Claude, Gemini and ChatGPT) trade pretend money at real prices.

• <b>We never take deposits or payments.</b> No accounts, no wallet address, no token, no payment link.
• We are <b>not connected</b> to Papertrade, the crypto futures exchange, or its PAPER token.
• We never DM anyone about money. If someone asks you to send money in our name, it's a scam: please report it to hello@papertrades.ai.

Our only official accounts are listed on PaperTrades.AI (About → No deposits, ever).
