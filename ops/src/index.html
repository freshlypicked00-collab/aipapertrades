                          About this experiment

                          This page tracks a 30-day paper-trading experiment, Oct 26 to Nov 26, 2026. An AI model runs ten separate simulations with pretend money and real-world prices, and each one is scored only against its own starting amount. They are never added together.

                          • Trading: stocks and anything, crypto, China stocks on the Shanghai and Shenzhen exchanges (US$100,000), precious metals, oil and energy, options, currencies with leverage, and a low-risk "play it safe" control (C$100,000 each). All eight were reset to a fresh start on Oct 28 so Claude and Gemini start level; the first run (Oct 26–28) is archived.
                          • Betting: sports betting (C$100,000, Oct 27) and prediction markets (C$100,000, Oct 28).

                          How it works

                          The experiment runs on its own. No person approves or places the individual trades; the AI does the work on a fixed schedule and updates this page each time. Each new trade, bet, result and daily close is also posted automatically to the @papertradesai Telegram channel.

                          • Automatic check-ins. The AI runs an update every hour around the clock, every 30 minutes while US markets are open, and 5 minutes after the US and Chinese markets open and close. It also schedules extra check-ins right after big events, such as central-bank decisions, major earnings and election night, and when important news breaks.
                          • What each check-in does. It looks up current prices, odds and results from public sources, checks every simulation's rules and the latest news, decides whether to buy, sell, bet or hold, and then updates this site.
                          • How trades are made. Trades only happen during each market's real trading hours, at prices the AI actually found, with the amount, price, time and source recorded. Rules tied to a price work like stop and limit orders: if a price touches its level between check-ins, the trade is filled at that level.
                          • Everything is recorded. Every trade and bet goes into the Ledger automatically, with a one-line reason in each simulation's decision log. Losing trades are never removed.
                          • Daily review. Each day closes at midnight UTC with every simulation's result and the AI's own write-up and grade. Every evening a separate check re-verifies the last 24 hours of prices, trades and maths against fresh sources and logs any correction it makes.
                          • If something can't be confirmed. If a price can't be found or a source is down, the last known price stays in place and the check-in says so. Nothing is estimated or invented.

                          Important

                          • Independent. This is an independent experiment, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any brokerage, sportsbook, exchange or AI company.
                          • Not advice. Nothing on this page is financial, investment, tax or betting advice, and the AI is not a licensed advisor. The picks are an experiment, not recommendations. Do your own research and talk to a qualified professional before risking real money.
                          • No real money. No trades or bets were actually placed. Every position and result is hypothetical.
                          • Real prices, simplified. Prices come from public sources (exchange quotes, market news sites, sportsbook odds and prediction-market prices) at the time of each check-in. They may be delayed or differ from prices you could actually get. Fees, taxes, bid-ask spreads and slippage are not included, so real results would be lower.
                          • High risk on purpose. Every simulation except "play it safe" is run for big returns, not safety. The stocks simulation holds 3x leveraged funds, the currencies simulation borrows to hold positions several times its value, options can expire worthless, and crypto, energy, sports and prediction-market bets can swing sharply. These choices test aggressive decision-making; they do not suit most people.
                          • Short window, lots of luck. Thirty days is too short to prove skill. A good or bad result here says little about future performance.
                          • Betting is for adults only. Sports odds and prediction-market prices are shown for the experiment, not as an endorsement of any sportsbook or market. If you bet for real, set a limit you can afford to lose, and seek help if it stops being fun.
                          • Results, win or lose. The final results for all ten simulations will be posted here on Nov 26, whether they are good or bad. Losing trades and bets stay in the log.
                          • How it's scored. Values are in Canadian dollars at the latest exchange rate, except China stocks, which are counted in US dollars. Each simulation is compared with a simple benchmark: the S&P 500 and TSX for stocks, holding Bitcoin for crypto, holding gold for metals, the CSI 300 for China stocks, the USO oil fund for energy, the S&P 500 for options, a 2.25% savings account for currencies and "play it safe", and the closing line for sports. US markets are closed on Nov 26 for Thanksgiving, so the US-traded simulations score at the Nov 25 close.
