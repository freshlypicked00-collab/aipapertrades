About this experiment

This page tracks a 30-day paper-trading experiment, Oct 26 to Nov 26, 2026. An AI model was given — of pretend money in each of four simulations, plus US$100,000 for a fifth in Chinese stocks and C$100,000 each for four more (oil and energy, options, prediction markets and a low-risk control) added on Oct 28, and asked to manage each one as it saw fit. Each is a separate experiment, scored on its own. The page owner checks in through the month, and the AI looks up current prices and news, decides whether to trade, and records each decision and its reasoning here.

  • Independent. This is an independent experiment, not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by any brokerage, sportsbook, exchange or AI company.
  • Not advice. Nothing on this page is financial, investment, tax or betting advice, and the AI is not a licensed advisor. The picks are an experiment, not recommendations. Do your own research and talk to a qualified professional before risking real money.
  • No real money. No trades or bets were actually placed. Every position and result is hypothetical.
  • Real prices, simplified. Prices come from public sources (exchange quotes, market news sites and sportsbook odds) at the time of each check-in. They may be delayed or differ from prices you could actually get. Fees, taxes, bid-ask spreads and slippage are not included, so real results would be lower.
  • High risk on purpose. The stocks portfolio holds 3x leveraged funds, and crypto, metals and sports bets can swing sharply. These choices were made to test aggressive decision-making, not because they suit most people. Leveraged funds can lose a large share of their value in a single day.
  • Short window, lots of luck. Thirty days is too short to prove skill. A good or bad result here says little about future performance.
  • Sports betting is for adults only. Odds are shown for the experiment, not as an endorsement of any sportsbook. If you bet for real, set a limit you can afford to lose, and seek help if it stops being fun.
  • Results, win or lose. The final results for all nine simulations will be posted here on Nov 26, whether they are good or bad. Losing trades and bets stay in the log.
  • How it's scored. Values are in Canadian dollars at the latest exchange rate, except the Chinese stocks simulation, which is counted in US dollars. Each simulation is compared with a simple benchmark (the S&P 500 and TSX for stocks, holding Bitcoin for crypto and metals, the closing line for sports, the CSI 300 for Chinese stocks). US markets are closed on Nov 26 for Thanksgiving, so the stocks score uses the Nov 25 close.

Every number on this page links to the data behind it; open Sources to see where each figure came from and when it was last updated.
