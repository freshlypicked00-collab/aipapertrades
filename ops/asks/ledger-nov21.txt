  - "All races" (the opening view): the Ledger exactly as it was.
  - A race: the ten race cards give way to that one race: its own numbers (last 24 hours, worst drop, last trade, vs. its benchmark: the same lines as its card), its Value over time chart, the pie and the "What <AI> holds in <race> now" table (the race pages' own parts, pointed at that race), and a link to the race's page.
