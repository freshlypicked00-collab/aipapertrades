M4 Line length on a computer. Many paragraphs run 150 to 200 characters per line (comfortable reading is 60 to 90).

CLAUDE'S PROPOSALS (each is small and can be reversed on its own):
READING
P1 No text under 12 px anywhere (labels, tags, chips, IDs all come up to 12 to 12.5).
P2 Phones: captions from 12.5 to 13 px and running text to 15 px; long explanations keep their "Show more" folds so pages do not get longer than about 5 percent.
P3 Computers: running text and notes no wider than about 85 characters per line; tables and charts keep the full width.
P4 Sentences inside tables and logs (reasons, notes, grades' comments) in the normal sans font; monospace only for numbers, prices, odds, times, tickers and transaction IDs. This also saves width.
TOUCH AND CONTROLS
P5 Phones: main buttons and dropdowns at least 40 px tall; small chips and close buttons keep their look but get an invisible larger touch area (at least 36 px).
P6 One style for every dropdown and button (the three default-grey ones fixed); one focus ring for keyboard users; the same hover and pressed feedback everywhere (150 ms).
P7 Contrast fixed to at least 4.5:1: slightly deeper Telegram blue, YouTube red and LIVE red under white text, dark text on the "Worst" tag (or a deeper red), same family of colours, no new colours.
LAYOUT
P8 Landing: a tab opened from a link, a reload or the menu always starts with its title fully visible under the sticky bar (computer and phone).
P9 Overview top, computer and tablet, same content, tidier: (a) the six settings (language, app, sound, Beginner, theme, full screen) become one compact right-aligned toolbar on the logo row; (b) one status line: Day 22 of 32, countdown, New York time, markets, last update, system status; Hong Kong and UTC clocks smaller on the same line; (c) the LIVE alerts box becomes one row: text and latest move on the left, the five buttons on the right, "Get notified" and "System status" as small links under the text. Expected: about 250 px less before the first race number, no ragged wrapping on tablets.
P10 Phone: a floating "Menu" button bottom-right next to "Top" (bottom-left) once you scroll, so any race is one tap away from anywhere.
P11 AI vs AI "Holding now": left-aligned, one holding per line, no stray dots.
P12 Bet and move rows: the chips (transaction ID, result link, game time, Whole story, More details) sit on one or two wrapped lines instead of 6 to 8 stacked ones; the competitor's view keeps its coloured bar.
P13 Hide a card when it is empty ("Coming up" with nothing scheduled).
P14 Footer: one row of links (Overview, Ledger, How it works, Rules & Audit, Guide, About, Support, Official accounts) above the legal text; text left-aligned in a readable column.
FEEL
P15 Quiet motion: 150 ms colour and border transitions on buttons, chips, tabs and clickable cards; nothing moves for people who turned animations off.
P16 Tables: row highlight on hover, column headers that stay visible while a long table scrolls sideways on a phone, a soft edge shadow that shows a table can be scrolled.
P17 Themed scrollbars and text selection in the accent colour; fold-outs get a clear chevron that turns when open.
P18 All eight colour themes checked after the changes (four dark, four light).
