WORDING FIXES in the shared move lists (High risk, the three AI pages, AI vs AI, the new race lists), found while building. Numbers are unchanged; only the words.
W1. A buy that was sold later said "open" in the Profit column. Example on High risk today: Gemini's Nov 16 buy of Direxion Daily Semiconductor Bear 3X Shares says "open", but it was sold on Nov 17 (+C$903.14 on the sale's own row). New: "sold" (a short: "bought back"; an option or prediction share that ran out: "expired" / "settled"). "open" only while it is still held. The profit stays on the sale's row only.
W2. Shorts. Money said "put in C$190,000.00" on the short and "took out C$190,812.45" on buying it back, next to a LOSS of C$812.45 (and "took out C$39,751.38" of C$40,000 next to a PROFIT of C$248.62). New: "sold short C$190,000.00" and "bought back for C$190,812.45".
W3. A cancelled (void) bet said "refunded C$0.00". New: the stake that came back, e.g. "refunded C$10,000.00".
W4. Fines. "Refund Penalty refunded (outage day) · C$500.00" becomes "Penalty refunded (outage day) · refunded C$500.00". "Penalty Missed daily betting minimum · C$500.00" becomes "Penalty: missed daily betting minimum · fined C$500.00".
W5. AI vs AI, "Recent trades": Claude's bets were missing (only its trades were listed); now they are in. Claude's rows now show its own reason, as in the Ledger, instead of the log line that first repeats "Bought X: C$… at US$…".

FOUND IN THE LEDGER, NOT CHANGED YET (your view decides)
L1. A buy that was added to and then sold says "Still held" and "open". Example: Claude's Oct 28 SPY buy (C-STK-002): it was sold on Nov 5 together with the Nov 4 add, and its own row has the button "View sale C-STK-005". Cause: the Ledger only looks at sales that come before the next buy of the same holding.
L2. The result lands on the wrong buy. The Nov 4 SPY add of C$15,000 shows "Lost −C$1,481.18 (sold)", which is the loss on the whole C$40,000 position. Claude's Oct 28 Bitcoin buy of C$35,000 shows "Lost −C$262.30 (sold)", which is only the Oct 29 part-sale; the rest lost C$1,899.40 on Nov 5.
L3. A holding sold completely and bought again later: the OLD buys say "Part sold …; rest still held" (Claude's USO: all sold Nov 2, bought again Nov 11).
Claude's proposal: treat the buys and sales of one holding as one POSITION, from the first buy until it is completely sold. (a) Every buy of a finished position says "Sold at a profit / at a loss" by the whole position's result, never "Still held". (b) In the Profit column a buy of a finished position shows the whole position's result once in words that say so: "Lost −C$1,481.18 (sold; whole position, 2 buys)"; a position with one buy keeps today's "Lost −C$… (sold)". (c) "Still held" and "Part sold …; rest still held" only on buys of a position that is still open now. Sales rows do not change.
